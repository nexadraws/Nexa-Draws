How do I enter a competition?

Choose a competition, select the number of entries you want and answer the skill question correctly to continue.

How will I know if I win?

Winners will be contacted using the details provided with their entry and published on our Winners page.

When does a competition close?

Each competition displays its closing date and entry availability. Check the individual competition for full details.

Why is there a skill question?

Entrants must answer the skill question correctly as part of the competition entry process. Full requirements are set out in the competition terms.
