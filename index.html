FAQs

Is this connected to real payments?

No. Checkout is deliberately demo-only. Connect a payment provider only after they approve your exact prize-competition model.

Where is the data stored?

For this prototype, data is saved with localStorage in this browser. Production use needs a real server-side database, authentication and backups.

Can I edit competitions myself?

Yes. Open the Admin button in the footer to create, edit, pause, publish winners or delete demo competitions.

What about a free-entry route and legal pages?

Templates are included as placeholders. Have the final wording reviewed for your business model and jurisdiction before accepting real entries.
